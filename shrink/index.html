Shrink

Make photos smaller, right in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Your photos never leave this device.

What are they for?

    Private by design

    Your browser does all the work. No server ever sees your photos, so it is safe for passports, contracts and family pictures.

    Clean files

    Location and camera details are removed, and iPhone photos keep the right way up.

    Never bigger

    If a photo is already small, you get the original back instead of a worse copy.
