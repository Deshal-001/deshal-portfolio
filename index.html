About me

I'm from Sri Lanka, an island where the sea is never far away, and I now live in Hildesheim. I like the whole path of an app: the screen someone taps, the API it calls and the database underneath.

Most of my work is Flutter with Clean Architecture and BLoC, backed by Spring Boot or Node.js on PostgreSQL. I've also trained and shipped image classifiers, and I care about explaining what a model sees, not only what it predicts.

Away from the keyboard I play cricket and football, and I photograph landscapes and streets as Peaceful Lens.
